PostgreSQL Audit CRUD Verification
==================================

Test database:
capstone_rls_test

Test performed:
Inserted, updated, and deleted a temporary student record
inside a transaction.

Verified audit events:
- INSERT: new row values captured in new_row.
- UPDATE: old_row and new_row captured.
- DELETE: deleted row values captured in old_row.
- All three events recorded changed_by and at.

Result:
All three audit operations were verified successfully.
The transaction was rolled back after verification.
