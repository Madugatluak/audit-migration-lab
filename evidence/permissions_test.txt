PostgreSQL Least-Privilege Permission Verification
=================================================

Test database:
capstone_rls_test

Role tested:
app_reader (NOLOGIN)

Verified results:
- SELECT on public.students: allowed
- SELECT on public.audit_log: denied
- INSERT on public.audit_log: denied

Audit trigger verification:
- INSERT into students created an audit_log entry.
- The entry captured the operation, current user, and new row values.
- The test transaction was rolled back.

Scope:
The audit trigger was verified while connected as postgres.
Ordinary application-writer trigger behavior has not yet been tested.
